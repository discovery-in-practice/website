Publication and reuse status

Our original article content is available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0). You may share and adapt it, including commercially, with attribution, a license link, and an indication of changes. Credit the author named on each article and Discovery in Practice, with the canonical article link. Linked third-party publications retain their own terms. Search, retrieval, and AI training access are permitted under the applicable license. The GitHub mirror contains the release files. A Hugging Face mirror is being prepared.

Keep the context

Retain the article version, attribution, references, and qualifications when reusing this material. Check the canonical article for corrections. References to other publications do not relicense those publications.
