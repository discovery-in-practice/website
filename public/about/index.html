About Discovery in Practice

Practical knowledge.
Shared openly.

Discovery in Practice is a resource for scientists developing, running, and interpreting assays in drug discovery.

We bring together more than 65 years of combined experience across drug discovery, pharmacology, high-throughput screening, detection technologies, and assay optimization.

Our aim is to make that experience useful to bench scientists, core laboratories, assay-development and automation teams, and early-stage biotechs.
